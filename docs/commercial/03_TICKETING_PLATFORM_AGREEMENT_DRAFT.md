## 1. Platform

The Platform Provider makes available the MnM Tickets platform for authorised event resale, ticket issuance, QR admission, refunds, reporting and audit.

## 2. Organiser authority

The Operator must record documented authority from each event organiser before publishing an event. The Operator is responsible for:
- the lawfulness of the resale and the price;
- the accuracy of event, venue, date, category and capacity data;
- honouring admission and refund obligations;
- resolving organiser/customer disputes.

The Platform Provider does not grant organiser rights and does not verify the underlying commercial authority beyond the recorded reference.

## 3. Capacity and sales

The platform reserves inventory during checkout and enforces paid capacity. The Operator must not manually restock beyond the organiser's authorised allocation. Capacity, per-order limits, sale windows and category prices are set by authorised staff.

## 4. Payments and settlement

Live payment collection is `[ACTIVATED / NOT ACTIVATED]`. Until activation, the development simulator must remain clearly labelled and must not be presented as real money.

Settlement account, payout timing, reconciliation method, chargeback responsibility, refund funding and fees are `[TO BE AGREED]`.

## 5. Refunds and cancellation

The Operator defines the customer refund policy, subject to law and organiser terms. The platform supports refund initiation/completion and event cancellation that voids tickets, releases holds and queues refunds. The Operator remains responsible for actually returning funds and communicating with customers.

## 6. Admission and scanning

Tickets are admitted once; duplicate or invalid scans are rejected and logged. The Operator must provide trained scanners and a fallback procedure for device/network failure. The Platform Provider does not guarantee connectivity at the venue.

## 7. Reporting and audit

The platform provides order, sales, commission, refund and scan records. The Operator is responsible for reconciling platform reports with its bank/provider statements. Audit logs must not be altered to conceal transactions.
